The layer where hardware, protocols & software meet.

I'm a final-year Computer Systems & Network Engineering undergraduate at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT). My work spans enterprise network design, performance optimisation on embedded devices, containerised infrastructure services, and cloud/IoT platforms on AWS.

I enjoy understanding how infrastructure fits together, from routing, switching, VLANs and wireless to virtual machines, Linux services, identity and security platforms. I like problems with a measurable answer: a latency budget to hit, an address plan that has to scale, a service that has to come up cleanly every time.
